01

Castle health, both builds

Two charts rather than one with two vertical scales: health peaks at 78,000 and incoming damage at 29,990, so a shared axis would flatten one and invent a crossing point that means nothing.

The shape changed, not just the numbers

The old line is a cliff — a tall repair-funded spike to 78,000 and then 24,045 health gone in a single second. The new line never gets that high and never falls that fast: its worst moment is three times shallower, and it grinds down through three similar windows instead of being deleted in one.

Peak incoming damage also dropped by a third, from 29,990 to 19,750. That is the wipes landing — the enemy army is being cut down rather than accumulating unopposed.
