04

What this says about our bot

The defence-only bot spent $85,229 on units in its mirror game. Marc spent $356 in his — a factor of 239. Both were handed the same gadget, on the same cooldown, spawning the same five tier-appropriate units per cast.

Earlier in this investigation the bot's tier-7 buying looked like a wiper-selection problem, and the fix for it — pricing a purchase against what is already deployed — came out win-rate neutral. This is a plausible reason why. The question it was answering was which unit to buy, in a matchup where the strongest player in the dataset buys twice and puts everything else on the ladder.

One caveat on how far to read this: it is a single game, and Marc's opponent here is the search bot rather than the HeuristicBot the defence-only measurements use. What transfers is the shape — the spend ratio and the relative ladder speed — not the exact numbers.
